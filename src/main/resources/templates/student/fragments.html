Consent to CCTV Recording and Use

Sejong University is committed to complying with the personal information protection provisions of the Personal Information Protection Act and other relevant laws, and to doing its utmost to protect the personal information of its users. Pursuant to Article 15(1) of the Personal Information Protection Act, we hereby inform you that your personal information may be collected and used as described below while you use Sejong University. Please give your consent accordingly.

Consent to the Collection and Use of Personal Information

Items Collected
CCTV footage recorded within the facilities
Purpose of Collection and Use
Student safety, facility inspection, and prevention of theft and crime, etc.
Period of Retention and Use
Retained for 60 days and then destroyed
How to Request Access
Contact and apply to the Office of Construction and Development, Sejong University (02-3408-3100)

You have the right to refuse consent to the provision of the above video information. However, if you refuse, the information you receive from the University may be restricted.

CCTV 촬영 및 이용 동의서
